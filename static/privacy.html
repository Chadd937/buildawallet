Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

What this covers

BuildAWallet provides a HUMAN wallet design experience and read-only Base and Solana mainnet data through its website, API, MCP interface and x402 endpoints. This policy describes the information processed by those services. Cloudflare Access requires a verified email sign-in before HUMAN pages, payments, builder design endpoints and saved designs can be opened. The landing page and machine data API remain public. The public service does not create or hold private keys, collect seed phrases, or sign transactions on your behalf. Do not enter secrets in a design, chat prompt, saved blueprint or support request.

Your designs and browser storage

The builder and studio keep drafts and preferences in your browser's local storage. The payment page holds a wallet login session token in that tab's session storage. Cloudflare Access uses an authentication cookie for the signed-in HUMAN session. We use this storage for the features you request; the site does not include advertising cookies, ad pixels or third-party analytics scripts. You can clear local storage in your browser to remove local drafts and close the payment tab to clear its session storage.

When you use the architect chat, we send the text and design state you submit to our HUMAN API to generate a reply. When you choose Save, we store the design and its random share code in our database; anyone with that link who signs in through Cloudflare Access can view it. A design appears in the public gallery only if you explicitly choose to publish it. Premium blueprint generation receives your submitted design to produce a response and does not store that design in the subscription tables. The save feature does not ask for your email address.

Payments, authentication and API use

To authenticate and provide paid access, our database stores your public paying wallet address and chain, one-use challenge and expiry, a hash of the session token, payment transaction ID and amount, plan and expiry, a hash of any issued API key, and metered usage counts. We use these records to verify wallet control and payment, prevent replay, restore access, and enforce plan limits. An API key is displayed once; the database stores its hash. A signed login message proves control of an address and does not authorize a token transfer.

For wallet, token and transaction lookups, you send a public address or transaction identifier. Our Worker uses the configured chain RPC providers to fetch public blockchain data. Per-request payments also use an x402 facilitator to verify and settle a payment. We do not use these queries to create an advertising profile.

Operational data and recipients

Cloudflare hosts the site, Workers, database and Access login. Access processes your email address, sign-in event, session cookie and related security metadata. The page and payment Worker verifies the signed Access token and checks for an email claim, but does not store that email in the subscription database. Cloudflare may also process IP addresses, request and security metadata, and diagnostic logs when delivering and protecting the service. Our configured Base and Solana RPC providers receive the public chain queries needed to answer your request or verify payment. The x402 payment facilitator receives payment verification data for a pay-per-request call. Your wallet provider handles the wallet connection and signatures you approve. Opening a chain explorer link sends your browser to that separate site. Public blockchain transfers, addresses and transaction details can remain visible to anyone, independently of BuildAWallet.

We use information to serve requests, secure the service, meter usage, handle payment issues and meet applicable obligations. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not embed third-party analytics or advertising trackers in the public pages or API reference.

Retention and control

Local drafts remain in your browser until you clear them. Saved designs remain available through their links to signed-in users, and published designs remain in the gallery unless removed. Challenge and session records carry expiry timestamps; payment, entitlement and usage records remain in our database for operating paid access, preventing duplicate redemption, resolving disputes and meeting applicable obligations. Expiry alone does not delete a record. Infrastructure logs follow the hosting provider's retention settings. Public chain records are outside our control and generally cannot be erased.

You can avoid saving or publishing a design, clear your local browser data, stop using the service, and revoke or rotate an API key through the payment flow. For a request about a saved design or paid account, include the share code or public paying wallet address and transaction ID, but never a seed phrase, private key, session token or API key. We may need proof of control before changing account records. Applicable law may give you access, correction, deletion, objection or other privacy rights, subject to verification and legal exceptions.

Security, children and international access

We use wallet message challenges, hashed bearer credentials, rate limits and payment verification to protect the service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. The service is not directed to children under 13. Cloudflare and chain providers may process data in countries outside your own.

Changes and contact

We will revise this page when our practices change and update the date above. For a non-sensitive product or privacy question, use the project's issue tracker. Issues are public: do not post personal data or credentials there. To request access, correction or deletion, open an issue with only a public share code or public wallet address you choose to disclose. Do not include private account data in a public issue; verification or a private response may require another channel.
