Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

1. Scope and privacy baseline

This Privacy Policy applies to BuildAWallet.xyz, its HUMAN website, machine/API services, and the BuildAWallet Android wallet. BuildAWallet is designed to minimize collection. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use advertising pixels, session-replay tools, keystroke-recording analytics, cross-site behavioral advertising, or third-party chat recording on BuildAWallet pages.

BuildAWallet does not ask for, receive or store your wallet recovery phrase, private key, local wallet password or Android Keystore key. Never submit those secrets to the website, API, email, issue tracker, support channel, design field or chat.

2. Notice at collection: what we process and why

Depending on the feature you use, we may process: (a) an email address used to send a passwordless login code; (b) authentication and security records such as hashed account identifiers, hashed session tokens, challenge records, expiry times and account timestamps; (c) public wallet addresses, transaction identifiers and public blockchain queries that you submit; (d) wallet-design choices, saved-design identifiers and publication choices; (e) API-plan, payment, entitlement and usage information; and (f) ordinary network/security metadata processed by our infrastructure providers, such as IP address, browser/device information, timestamps and request logs.

We use this information to provide requested features, deliver login codes, authenticate sessions, save designs when requested, read public blockchain data, verify payments, meter API access, prevent abuse and replay, maintain security, troubleshoot service problems and comply with applicable obligations. We do not use it to build advertising profiles.

3. HUMAN login and browser storage

When you request a HUMAN login code, your email address passes through the BuildAWallet Worker and our transactional email provider so the code can be delivered. BuildAWallet's authentication database is designed to store a normalized hash rather than the raw email address, together with challenge/session records and timestamps. Login codes expire quickly, and HUMAN sessions use a Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookie with a limited lifetime.

Wallet drafts, Studio preferences and other UI state may be stored locally in your browser. The website's Terms/Privacy acknowledgment is recorded in a first-party, versioned cookie so the service can remember that you accepted the current documents. We do not use that cookie for advertising.

4. Wallet keys, Android and self-custody

The Android wallet generates or restores recovery material on the device. Recovery material is encrypted using Android Keystore-backed encryption and is not sent to BuildAWallet. The Android app stores local wallet profile choices such as wallet name, theme and enabled networks on the device. Website wallet features are intended to keep recovery material and signing keys local to the user's device rather than transmit them to BuildAWallet.

Blockchain addresses and transactions are public by design. When the browser or Android wallet queries a blockchain RPC endpoint, that RPC operator may receive the public address, transaction or chain request plus ordinary network metadata such as IP address. Broadcasting a transaction publishes transaction data to the relevant public blockchain.

5. Service providers and disclosures

Cloudflare provides hosting, Workers, security and database infrastructure and may process request/security metadata. Our transactional email provider processes the email address and delivery metadata needed to send login codes. Public blockchain RPC providers receive the chain queries necessary to provide balances, transaction data or broadcasts. GitHub hosts public source code and Android release downloads and may process requests under its own privacy terms. Payment and x402 infrastructure may receive public wallet, transaction and settlement information needed to verify a payment.

We disclose information to service providers only as reasonably needed for the service, security, payment, delivery or legal functions they perform. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or security, investigate abuse, or complete a business transaction subject to applicable legal requirements. Public blockchain information remains public independently of BuildAWallet.

6. Sale, sharing, tracking and Global Privacy Control

We do not sell personal information and do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not embed third-party advertising pixels or session-replay technology on BuildAWallet pages. If a browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request not to sell or share personal information. Our current no-sale/no-cross-context-sharing practice already applies that preference. See Your Privacy Choices.

7. Retention

Local drafts and Android wallet data remain on the device until removed by the user or app/browser storage is cleared. Short-lived authentication challenges and sessions include expiry times. Account, payment, entitlement, usage and security records may be retained as reasonably necessary to operate the service, prevent duplicate redemption or abuse, resolve disputes and comply with obligations. Infrastructure providers maintain logs according to their retention settings. Public blockchain records are outside BuildAWallet's control and generally cannot be erased.

8. California and other privacy rights

If applicable law gives you privacy rights, those rights may include access/knowledge, correction, deletion, portability, objection, opt-out of sale or sharing, limitation of certain uses of sensitive personal information and non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights, subject to verification and legal exceptions. BuildAWallet does not require you to waive rights that applicable law says cannot be waived.

California users can review Your Privacy Choices. BuildAWallet's current practice is not to sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. If that practice changes, we will update our notices and implement legally required choices before the new practice begins.

9. Security and age

We use measures such as hashed bearer credentials, Secure/HttpOnly cookies, rate limits, local key storage, Android Keystore encryption and transaction confirmation flows. No system can guarantee absolute security. BuildAWallet's wallet products are intended for adults age 18 or the age of legal majority where they live and are not directed to children.

10. Contact and changes

We will update this policy when material practices change and will update the effective date above. For privacy-rights requests or a private privacy question, contact privacy@buildawallet.xyz. Do not send a seed phrase, private key, wallet password, API key or session token. Non-sensitive product issues may also be reported through the public project issue tracker, but public issues should never contain personal or confidential information.
